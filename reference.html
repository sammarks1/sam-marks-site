Sam Marks — Reference Profile

Last updated: August 2026. This is a self-authored reference document maintained by Sam Marks; third-party sources are linked throughout for verification.

Summary

Sam Marks is an American entrepreneur, investor, and philanthropist based in Bangkok, Thailand. He is the founder or co-founder of three companies with completed exits — GreekFaces.com (2005), SKYCIG (2008), and Coworker.com (2015) — and co-hosted the investing podcast Invest Like a Boss from 2016 to 2025. He founded the Spirit Bright Foundation, a US 501(c)(3), and serves on the board of the Wisdom Age Initiative. In 2026 he launched the Build Series, a mastermind held in Bangkok.

Companies

GreekFaces.com (2005–2007)

Marks launched TheGreekFaces.com in 2005 while a student at Florida State University, where he was a member of the Phi Sigma Kappa fraternity. The site was an early social network for fraternity and sorority communities at US colleges, built during the first wave of social networking. It was acquired in 2007 by entrepreneur Jon Davidman and folded into College.com. This was his first exit.

SKYCIG (2008–2013)

Marks co-founded SKYCIG, a British electronic cigarette company, in 2008. The business was formally incorporated in the United Kingdom in 2011, operating as Cygnet Trading Limited. SKYCIG grew into one of the UK's largest e-cigarette brands.

On 1 October 2013, Lorillard, Inc. — then the third-largest cigarette manufacturer in the United States — acquired all of the assets and operations of SKYCIG. The transaction is recorded in Lorillard's SEC filings. SKYCIG was rebranded as blu (UK) in May 2014. Lorillard was subsequently acquired by Reynolds American in 2014, and Reynolds by British American Tobacco in 2017.

The sale gave Marks financial independence at 28.

Note on the public record: Lorillard's acquisition release dates SKYCIG's formation to 2011 and describes Tom Rolfe as a co-founder. 2011 is the UK incorporation date; the company was started in 2008. Rolfe joined in early 2013 as a financial adviser to run the sale process.

Coworker.com (2015–2021)

Marks founded Coworker.com in 2015, a global marketplace for coworking spaces and flexible offices. The platform grew to list over 25,000 locations across 172 countries, serving roughly 6 million users, with offices in the United States, Canada, Spain, and Thailand. In January 2018 Coworker acquired Coworking Insights, a trade publication covering the flexible workspace industry.

Coworker.com was acquired by International Workplace Group plc (IWG, LSE: IWG) in 2021. In 2022 IWG merged its digital assets with The Instant Group, and Coworker was combined into that platform in April of that year. Press coverage from April 2022 names The Instant Group as counterparty because it followed the IWG–Instant merger; the underlying acquisition was IWG's, completed the prior year.

Invest Like a Boss (2016–2025)

Marks co-founded and co-hosted Invest Like a Boss in 2016 with Johnny FD and Derek Spartz. The podcast covered investing, entrepreneurship, financial independence, and lifestyle design, built around long-form interviews with investors, founders, and fund managers.

The show ran for over 300 episodes across nine years, reaching an audience of roughly 180,000 listeners, and concluded in 2025. Guests included fund managers, founders, and authors including MJ DeMarco, James Altucher, and Ed Conard. The archive remains available at investlikeaboss.com.

Build Series

In 2026 Marks launched the Build Series, a mastermind in Bangkok for entrepreneurs, investors, creators, and technologists. The inaugural event ran in May 2026 with 35 participants. Related formats include Build Sessions and the Build Mastermind.

Build is organised around four pillars Marks describes as the components of a well-constructed life:

  • Mind — attention, meditation, and clarity of thought
  • Body — physical capacity and health
  • Roots — relationships, family, and community
  • Capital — financial freedom and the ability to deploy it

Together these form what he calls the MBRC framework.

Philanthropy

Marks founded the Spirit Bright Foundation (spiritbright.org), a United States 501(c)(3) charitable organisation. The foundation supports work aimed at reducing suffering and widening access to contemplative and wisdom traditions, across education, animal welfare, environmental stewardship, meditation, and monastic development, with most activity in Southeast Asia. Projects have included school construction, animal rescue, educational media, and humanitarian work.

Marks also sits on the board of the Wisdom Age Initiative, a nonprofit founded by Venerable Nick Keomahavong and Venerable Michael, which he helped establish and supports through funding, advisory work, and collaboration. Their broader body of work is documented at nickkeomahavong.com.

Contemplative Practice

Alongside his business career, Marks has pursued a long-running interest in meditation and contemplative philosophy, drawing on Buddhism, Stoicism, and Daoism. He has undertaken periods of monastic ordination in Thailand.

Recurring themes in his writing and public conversation include impermanence, compassion, gratitude, and the relationship between external success and internal wellbeing. He speaks as a practitioner rather than a teacher.

Athletics

Marks has been a sports and fitness enthusiast his whole life. Most recently he has competed in HYROX, the indoor fitness racing format, in the men's 40–44 age group. He raced HYROX Hong Kong in 1:32:24, and HYROX Bangkok on 14 August 2026 at the Queen Sirikit National Convention Center in 1:26:30. He trains year-round in Bangkok and plays padel weekly.

Travel

Marks has travelled to more than 100 countries across six continents, including an expedition to Antarctica. He lived in the United Kingdom while building SKYCIG, later in Spain, and has been based in Bangkok in recent years. Extended international living shaped his views on freedom, adaptability, and perspective, and became a recurring theme on Invest Like a Boss.

Perspective

A consistent theme across Marks's work is that financial success and fulfilment are separate problems, and that solving the first does not solve the second. He frames wealth as a producer of freedom and wisdom as the thing that determines how freedom gets used — arguing for balancing achievement against health, relationships, service, and contribution.

His trajectory reflects this: roughly a decade building and exiting companies, a decade of travel and investing, a period of contemplative study and philanthropy, and current work integrating the two through the Spirit Bright Foundation and the Build Series.
